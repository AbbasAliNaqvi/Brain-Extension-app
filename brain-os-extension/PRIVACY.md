Brain Extension Privacy Policy

Data Collection: We collect text highlighted by the user, current tab URLs, and screenshots (Snap & Learn) only when a feature is explicitly triggered.

User-Initiated Saving: Users may choose to save specific insights, code snippets, or memories to their "Second Brain." This data is stored in our database only with the user's direct consent (clicking "Save").

Automation & Remote Access: The extension may navigate or interact with web pages to perform autonomous tasks as directed by the user.

Data Usage: All data is sent securely to brain-extension-exng.onrender.com to be processed by AI models (Groq/LLM).

Storage: We use chrome.storage.local for tokens and local settings. User "memories" are stored securely in our cloud database for retrieval.

Third Parties: We do not sell data. Information is only shared with AI infrastructure providers to generate technical responses.
