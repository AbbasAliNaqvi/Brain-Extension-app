Privacy Policy for Brain Extension
Last Updated: March 4, 2026

Brain Extension is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you understand how your data is handled. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used to provide you with a seamless AI-powered browsing experience.

1. Information We Collect
To fulfill the purpose of an AI-driven productivity assistant, we collect the following types of information:

User-Selected Content: We collect text, images (via "Snap & Learn"), and media transcripts that you explicitly select or choose to send to the extension for AI analysis, explanation, or translation.

Authentication Data: We store secure authentication tokens in your local browser environment to maintain your session and sync your personal knowledge database across devices.

Usage Statistics: We track basic usage data (such as daily save counts or streak information) to provide you with productivity insights.

Browser Metadata: For features like "AI ContextOS," we access the titles and URLs of your open tabs solely to organize them into project-based workspaces.

2. Third-Party AI Service Providers
Brain Extension leverages third-party artificial intelligence models to process your requests.

Data Transfer: When you request an action (e.g., summarizing an article or generating an architecture diagram), the relevant text or context is transmitted securely to our AI service providers, including Google Gemini and Groq.

Purpose: This transfer occurs only to fulfill your specific request. The providers process this data to generate the AI responses you see in the extension panel.

Compliance: We ensure that our data transfer practices comply with the Chrome Web Store’s "Limited Use" requirements.

3. Data Security
Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers (or third-party AI providers) is encrypted via secure API connections.

Local Storage: Sensitive information, such as your authentication tokens and workspace preferences, is stored securely within your browser's local storage and is never exposed to public access.

4. Your Rights and Choices
No Selling of Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

Data Deletion: You can delete your saved content at any time through the extension's settings or by clearing your workspace data.

Revoking Access: You can stop all data collection immediately by uninstalling the extension or revoking access permissions in your browser settings.

5. Changes to this Policy
We may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in our features or compliance requirements. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this document will indicate when these changes take effect.
